# 教学资料摘要示例

原创练习材料改写，不是真实新闻。

资料A：学生用时间和动词短语介绍作息，同伴记录时间并提问。

资料B：阅读任务先圈时间，再排序事件，最后写自己的作息。

资料C：观察记录提示需要补充第三人称动词形式练习，仍需真实课堂证据核实。

## School Day Reading

Our class is reading about a school day. First, we look at the title and talk about our day. Then we read the story and find the times. We put the activities in order. We work with a friend and check our answers. After that, we write two sentences about our own day. We can use “I get up at seven.” We read our sentences to a friend. Our friend can ask a question. Reading and talking help us learn together.

词语：title 标题；first 首先；then 然后；activity 活动；together 一起。

问题1：What do we find in the story? 答案：The times.

问题2：How many sentences do we write about our own day? 答案：Two.
