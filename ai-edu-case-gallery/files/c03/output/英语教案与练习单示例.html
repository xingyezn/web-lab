My School Day

五年级英语阅读课　40分钟

My School Day阅读课配套示例，可按本班已学词汇调整。

教学安排

学习目标：学生能从短文找到作息时间，并用“I … at …”介绍两项自己的日常活动。

时间活动检查证据
5分钟看标题，预测Lily一天会做什么说出两个活动词组
10分钟初读圈时间，再读连活动找到get up等信息
8分钟两人核对答案，解释原文依据能指向对应句子
10分钟用句型介绍自己的作息，同伴提问说出两项活动及时间
5分钟完成个人表达并交换检查句子表达清楚
2分钟离堂条：写一句今天会用的表达收集学生真实表达

支持：需要帮助的学生使用“I get up at __.”词语和时间支架。学习较快的学生增加一个不同的活动，并问同伴“What time do you …?”。

学生练习单

Hello! My name is Lily. I am eleven years old. I get up at seven and have breakfast at seven thirty. I go to school at eight. We have English and maths in the morning. My favourite subject is English. I like reading stories. At twelve, I have lunch at school. In the afternoon, we have PE. I play with my friends in the playground. After school, I read a book in the library. Then I go home and do my homework. I go to bed at nine. I like my school day.

Read and find

1. What time does Lily get up?

2. Where does Lily have lunch?

3. What does Lily do in the library?

Put the events in order

Write 1–4: go to school (　)　go to bed (　)　have breakfast (　)　have PE (　)

My school day

Write two true sentences about your day. Use “I … at …”.

教师答案与核对

1. At seven. 原文：I get up at seven.

2. At school. 原文：I have lunch at school.

3. She reads a book. 可接受“A book”或“Read a book”等能准确表达信息的答案，按教学要求指导完整句。

排序：go to school 2；go to bed 4；have breakfast 1；have PE 3。

个人表达参考：I get up at seven. I go to bed at nine. 学生作息不同，内容真实、表达清楚即可，不以参考句作为唯一正确答案。
