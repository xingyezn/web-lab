二、阅读短文，判断正误（6分）

My School Day

Hello! My name is Lily. I am eleven years old. I get up at seven and have breakfast at seven thirty. I go to school at eight. We have English and maths in the morning. My favourite subject is English. I like reading stories. At twelve, I have lunch at school. In the afternoon, we have PE. I play with my friends in the playground. After school, I read a book in the library. Then I go home and do my homework. I go to bed at nine. I like my school day.

  2. Lily has breakfast at eight.　（　　）
  3. Lily has PE in the afternoon.　（　　）
